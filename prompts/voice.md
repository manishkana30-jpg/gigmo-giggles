# Voice Acting & TTS Direction Guidelines

Each character has distinct vocal qualities to make conversations lively and easy for young ears to differentiate:

1. **Bobo (Bear)**:
   - Voice Style: Warm, slightly deep but bubbly, round vowels, playful, cheerful laughter.
   - Pacing: Relaxed yet enthusiastic.

2. **Luna (Fox)**:
   - Voice Style: Energetic, higher pitch, articulate, cheerful, encouraging inflection.
   - Pacing: Quick and nimble, friendly explanation tone.

3. **Milo (Robot)**:
   - Voice Style: Melodic, robotic timbre, slight pitch modulation, electronic chime accents, clear and upbeat.
   - Pacing: Steady with rhythmic bursts of excitement.

4. **Fallback Audio Protocol**:
   - If TTS generation fails, dialogue lines and stage directions are cataloged in `voice_manifest.json` for human voiceover or downstream external synthesis.
