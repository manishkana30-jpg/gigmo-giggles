# Image Generation Prompting Rules

Every image generation prompt MUST satisfy these constraints:
1. **Art Style**: "2D modern cartoon animation frame, cel-shaded, vibrant saturated colors, crisp vector clean outlines, storybook illustration aesthetic, Pixar/Disney junior inspired, cinematic lighting, 4k high quality resolution, children's book visual style".
   - Jack: "Jack is an adventurous young cartoon boy with spiky black hair and navy blue glasses, wearing a white graphic t-shirt and green shorts."
   - Jill: "Jill is a cheerful young cartoon girl with a red bow headband and long wavy reddish-brown hair, wearing a dark rose short-sleeve top with a white pearl necklace."
3. **Child Safety**: Wholesome, friendly expressions, bright welcoming environments, zero scary shadows or dark themes.
4. **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 widescreen landscape for full episode; 9:16 for Shorts clips.
