# Image Generation Prompting Rules

Every image generation prompt MUST satisfy these constraints:
1. **Art Style**: "2D modern cartoon animation frame, cel-shaded, vibrant saturated colors, crisp vector clean outlines, storybook illustration aesthetic, Pixar/Disney junior inspired, cinematic lighting, 4k high quality resolution, children's book visual style".
2. **Character Canonical Injections**:
   - Bobo: "Bobo the chubby honey-brown cartoon teddy bear with round fuzzy ears and sunny yellow neckerchief"
   - Luna: "Luna the bright orange cartoon fox with creamy white chest, amber eyes, and teal explorer vest"
   - Milo: "Milo the friendly sky-blue compact robot with lime-green glowing digital eyes and yellow antenna light"
3. **No Realistic Humans or Children**: Fictional anthropomorphic cartoon animals and friendly robots only.
4. **Child Safety**: Wholesome, friendly expressions, bright welcoming environments, zero scary shadows or dark themes.
5. **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 widescreen landscape for full episode; 9:16 for Shorts clips.
