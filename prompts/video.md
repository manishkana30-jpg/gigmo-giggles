# Video & Ken Burns Animation Directives

To assemble high-energy kids videos from comic frames without requiring expensive generative video APIs:
1. **Ken Burns Motions**:
   - `zoom_in`: Smooth focal zoom into the character's facial expression or discovery object (scale from 1.0 to 1.15).
   - `zoom_out`: Reveal the wider scene environment from an initial close-up (scale from 1.15 to 1.0).
   - `pan_left_right`: Smooth horizontal panning across panoramic diagrams or landscape scenes.
   - `pulse_bounce`: Subtle rhythmic scale pulses to simulate lively character speech beats.
2. **Transitions**:
   - `crossfade`: 0.5s soft dissolve between scenes.
   - `slide`: Quick 0.3s lateral push to maintain high kid engagement.
3. **Pacing & Audio Sync**:
   - Subtitle overlay at bottom center, large font with high-contrast outline.
   - Background music track mixed at 15% volume so dialogue remains crisp and intelligible.
