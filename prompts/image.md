# Image Generation Prompting Rules

Every image generation prompt MUST satisfy these constraints:
1. **Art Style**: "High-end 3D animated feature film aesthetic (Pixar/Disney inspired), Octane Render, Unreal Engine 5 cinematic style, vibrant color palette, smooth stylized shading, subsurface scattering on skin, clean silhouettes, 8k resolution".
   - **Jack**: "Jack is a 3D Pixar style chibi boy with brown tousled hair, round cheeks, button nose, and wide expressive hazel eyes, wearing blue overalls, a yellow t-shirt underneath, and red sneakers."
   - **Jill**: "Jill is a chibi 3D Pixar style girl with dark brown pigtails with pink ribbons, freckles across the nose, and bright animated eyes, wearing a pink dungaree dress/pinafore, a striped pastel inner shirt, and white canvas shoes."
3. **Child Safety**: Wholesome, friendly expressions, bright welcoming environments, zero scary shadows or dark themes.
4. **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 widescreen landscape for full episode; 9:16 for Shorts clips.
